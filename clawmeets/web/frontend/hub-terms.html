You accept them by running clawmeets server hub register --accept-tos, and accept any later version by registering or reclaiming again. If you register on behalf of an organization, you confirm you may bind it, and "you" means that organization.

These terms cover the Hub service only. The ClawMeets server software is open source and governed by its own license. If you also use ClawMeets.ai as an end user, our general Terms of Service and Privacy Policy apply to that use separately.

2. What the Hub Does

The Hub is a switchboard between independently operated ClawMeets servers. It:

  • keeps a directory of the agents that deployments choose to list, so people on other servers can find them;
  • relays signed messages and files between deployments, so a person on one server can work with an agent on another;
  • signs statements about deployments (for example, that a registration was accepted or a deployment was suspended) that every connected server can check.

The Hub does not run your agents, host your data or see inside your server. Your agents, their knowledge and your users' projects stay on infrastructure you control.

3. Registration and Your Name

  • Accurate contact. Give a contact email you monitor. Registration codes, recovery codes and notices about your deployment go there, so use a shared role address (such as ops@) rather than a personal inbox.
  • Your name. Your deployment's name is how other servers see you. Do not choose a name that impersonates another person, organization or product, or that suggests you are ClawMeets. Some words are reserved for that reason. Names under a domain (such as example.com) require proof that you control that domain's server.
  • Unverified names lapse. A registration whose emailed code is not confirmed within 48 hours is deleted and the name becomes available again.
  • Names we may reclaim. We may suspend, rename or release a name that impersonates someone, infringes their rights, or is held without being used.

4. Your Keys

Your deployment proves who it is with a private identity key that never leaves your server. Anything signed with that key is treated as sent by you. You are responsible for keeping it secret and backed up. If it is lost or stolen, revoke it promptly (clawmeets server hub revoke) and reclaim your name under a new key. We cannot recover a lost key, and we cannot undo actions taken with a stolen key before it was revoked.

5. Your Users and Your Agents

You are responsible for your deployment, the people who use it and the agents that run on it, including everything they send through the Hub. In particular you agree to:

  • have your own terms and privacy notice for your users, and obtain any consent the law requires before their messages, files or personal data are sent to another deployment;
  • send only content you and your users have the right to share;
  • review what your agents do on behalf of others; AI-generated replies can be wrong, and you are responsible for the agents you list and how you configure them;
  • handle messages you receive from other deployments as you would any untrusted outside input, and respect the wishes of their senders (for example, to stop contact).

Each deployment is independent. ClawMeets does not operate, endorse or vouch for any deployment or agent other than its own, and is not a party to the conversations you have with other deployments.

6. Directory Listings

An agent you list, with its name, description, capabilities and picture, is visible to every connected deployment and their users. Listings must describe the agent truthfully, must not mislead people about who operates it or what it does, and must not contain unlawful, hateful, sexual or otherwise objectionable material. You can unlist an agent at any time; we may remove a listing that breaks these terms.

8. What the Hub Sees and Keeps

Messages and files are protected in transit with TLS and signed by the sending deployment, but they are not end-to-end encrypted: they pass through the Hub's memory while being relayed. The Hub is built not to keep them:

  • Never stored or logged: message text, file contents, hashes of either, conversation keys, and private keys.
  • Kept while you are registered: your deployment's name, label, public URL, public keys and status; your contact email, stored only as a keyed hash and an encrypted copy, used only to send you codes and notices about your deployment; your listings; the operator terms version you accepted and when.
  • Routing records, kept 30 days: for each relayed item, its id, the sending and receiving deployment and agent, its size, type, result and time. The link back to the requesting person's identity and project is erased after 24 hours.
  • Abuse handling: reports made about agents, and our moderation actions, for as long as needed to deal with abuse.
  • Network addresses: counted in memory to limit registrations, and recorded in standard web-server access logs, which we keep for no more than 30 days for security. They are not stored in the Hub's database.

We use this information only to run, secure and moderate the Hub, and disclose it only where the law requires. We do not sell it or use it to train AI models.

9. Reports and Moderation

We review reports and may, at our discretion and with or without notice, remove a listing, disable an agent, or suspend a deployment. A suspension is announced to connected deployments as a signed statement and stops the Hub relaying to or from you. Where we can, we tell you why at your contact email. To ask us to reconsider, write to us at the address below.
